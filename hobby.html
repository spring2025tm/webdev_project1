The Ultimate Hobby

Below I have selected a few of my favorites from my current houseplant collection. It is a very calming pastime I picked up during the 2020 pandemic. It is the perfect mix of calming, low-effort, and challenging to that make it a great activity.

I love to take on new species and learn what makes them very happy or very, very sad. I indeed have lost a handful of plants in the name of experimentation and learning.

However, its important to note that I have learned a lot from my succeess and my failures. I now have a solid, growing understanding of indoor and outdoor plant care. Plant care can be very rewarding.

My Plants:

White Wizard Philodendron

Unique climbing plant with white, green, and burgundy varigated leaves. This was an all white leaf developing!

Begonia Rex "Silver"

An indoor version of an outdoor plant, still learning about this one.

Monstera Cobra

The highlight of a Monstera Cobra is the leaf shape and the white variegation.

Philodendron "Birkin"

True or False: I have 2 Birkins. Slow growing, but produces really pretty leaves with thick stems.

Golden Pothos

Oldest plant of my collection, waiting for the day it outgrows the giant pot it calls home."
